Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how Unitify handles information when you use our unit-conversion app, including information that may be processed by third-party advertising services.

Unitify Privacy Policy
Provided by Gavora Technologies
Effective date: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Unitify, a unit-conversion application provided by Gavora Technologies, handles information when you use the app or contact us for support.

1. Information stored on your device

Unitify performs conversions on your device and stores certain information locally to provide its features. This may include:

  • Preferences such as your selected country, category, units, unit system, decimal separator, and display settings.
  • Recent conversions, including entered values, calculated results, and associated units and categories.
  • Your favorite categories and favorite conversions.
  • Your response to the conversion disclaimer.

This information is used to restore your preferences, display conversion history, and provide personalized defaults.

Selecting a country within Unitify is an app preference. It does not give Unitify access to your device's precise geographic location.

2. Information transmitted or processed

Unitify does not require you to create an account. Gavora Technologies does not upload your conversion inputs, calculated results, favorites, conversion history, or locally stored preferences to servers operated by Gavora Technologies.

Unitify may use third-party services, including Google AdMob, to provide advertising. These third-party services may automatically collect or process certain information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Depending on the service, your device, your settings, applicable law, and your consent choices, information processed by an advertising provider may include:

  • IP address.
  • Device and advertising identifiers.
  • General device and operating-system information.
  • Advertising interactions and impressions.
  • Diagnostic and performance information.
  • Other information required to provide, secure, measure, or improve advertising services.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or other information you choose to provide.

We use support correspondence to respond to your request and provide assistance. Please avoid sending information that is unnecessary for resolving your issue.

3. Copying and sharing

When you choose a copy action, Unitify places the selected conversion information on your device's clipboard. Clipboard access and retention depend on your operating system and device settings.

When you choose to share, Unitify may prepare text or an image containing app content and open your device's sharing interface.

The information you choose to share may include conversion values, calculated results, units, and other content visible in the shared material.

The application or recipient you select receives the content that you choose to send. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy practices.

Images or other files prepared for sharing may be stored temporarily in the app's cache.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

Unitify may display advertisements provided through Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google.

Google and its advertising services may collect or process certain information from your device for purposes such as providing advertisements, measuring advertising performance, preventing fraud and abuse, and improving advertising services.

Depending on your device, region, consent choices, applicable law, and advertising configuration, this information may include device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, general device information, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information.

Where required by applicable law, users may be presented with consent choices before personalized advertising or certain advertising-related processing occurs.

Depending on your consent choices, age, location, device settings, and applicable requirements, Google may provide personalized, non-personalized, or limited ads.

Google Privacy Information

You can learn more about how Google handles information through its services by reviewing Google's Privacy Policy.

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Gavora Technologies does not sell or rent users' personal information.

5. Device access

Unitify's core conversion features do not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or photo library.

Creating an image for sharing captures content generated within Unitify. It does not use your device's camera.

Local storage, clipboard actions, and sharing use facilities provided by your device's operating system.

Third-party SDKs included in the application may access device or network information necessary to provide their services, subject to Android permissions, platform controls, applicable law, and their respective privacy policies.

6. Retention and deletion

Preferences, favorites, disclaimer choices, and conversion history may remain in Unitify's local storage while needed to provide the app's features.

Recent conversion history may be limited, and older entries may be automatically removed as new entries are saved.

You can remove locally stored Unitify data through your Android device's Settings. Typically, you can open Apps, select Unitify, choose Storage, and select Clear storage or Clear data. Menu names may vary by device.

Uninstalling Unitify normally removes data stored locally by the application.

Operating-system backup and restore features may preserve or restore app data depending on your device settings. These backups are controlled through your device or backup provider.

Clearing Unitify's local data does not delete content that you have already copied, shared, or saved in another application or service.

Support emails may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and meet applicable legal obligations.

You may contact us to request deletion of personal information contained in correspondence you have sent directly to Gavora Technologies.

Gavora Technologies cannot remotely access or delete information stored only on your device.

Information processed independently by third-party services is subject to the retention and deletion practices of those service providers.

7. Security

Local app data is stored using storage facilities provided to Unitify by your operating system.

The protection of locally stored information also depends on your device security, screen lock, operating-system configuration, and backup settings.

We take reasonable steps to protect information under our control, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

8. Third-party services and external links

Unitify may use or interact with third-party services, including Google AdMob and services provided by your Android device.

Unitify may also open websites, an email application, an app store, or a sharing destination when you choose those actions.

Third-party services operate according to their own terms and privacy policies. Gavora Technologies does not control how independent third-party services process information.

9. Children's privacy

Unitify is a general-purpose unit-conversion utility and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to Gavora Technologies through support correspondence, please contact us so that we can review the request and take appropriate action.

Advertising shown to users may be subject to additional requirements based on the user's age, applicable law, Google Play policies, and Google advertising policies.

10. Your choices

Depending on your device, region, and applicable law, you may have choices concerning advertising personalization and advertising-related data processing.

These choices may be available through consent messages presented within the app, your Android device settings, your Google account settings, or other controls provided by Google.

You may also clear Unitify's locally stored data or uninstall the application at any time.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Unitify's features, third-party services, advertising configuration, legal requirements, or data practices change.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

Where required, we may provide additional notice or request consent.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or information you have provided directly to Gavora Technologies, please contact us.
