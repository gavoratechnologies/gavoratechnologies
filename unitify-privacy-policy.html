Unitify Privacy Policy

Provided by Gavora Technologies
Effective date: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Unitify, a unit-conversion app provided by Gavora Technologies, handles information when you use the app or contact us for support.

1. Information stored on your device

Unitify performs conversions on your device and stores information locally to provide its features. This includes:

  • Preferences such as your selected country, category, units, unit system, decimal separator, and display settings.
  • Recent conversions, including entered values, calculated results, and the associated units and categories.
  • Your favorite categories and favorite conversions.
  • Your response to the conversion disclaimer.

This information is used to restore your preferences, display conversion history, and provide personalized defaults. Selecting a country is a preference; it does not give the app access to your device's location.

2. Information transmitted to us

The current version of Unitify does not require an account and does not upload your conversion inputs, results, history, or preferences to servers operated by Gavora Technologies. It does not include analytics, remote crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs.

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments or other information you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request and provide support. Please avoid sending information that is unnecessary for resolving your issue.

3. Copying and sharing

When you choose a copy action, Unitify places the selected conversion information on your device's clipboard. Clipboard access and retention depend on your operating system and settings.

When you choose to share, Unitify prepares text or an image of app content and opens your device's sharing interface. The information you share may include conversion values, results, and other content visible in the shared image.

The app or recipient you select receives the content you choose to send. Their handling of that content is governed by their own privacy practices. Images prepared for sharing may temporarily be stored in the app's cache.

4. Advertising and sale of information

The current version does not serve advertisements through an advertising network. Any advertisement space shown in the interface is a placeholder and does not load third-party ads. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

5. Device access

Unitify's current features do not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo library. Creating an image for sharing captures app content; it does not use your camera.

Local storage, clipboard actions, and sharing use facilities provided by your device's operating system.

6. Retention and deletion

Preferences, favorites, disclaimer choices, and conversion history remain in the app's local storage while needed for its features. Recent history is limited, and older entries may be automatically removed as new entries are saved.

You can remove locally stored app data through Android's Settings by opening Apps, selecting Unitify, and choosing Storage and Clear storage or Clear data. Menu names vary by device. Uninstalling the app normally removes its local data.

Operating-system backup and restore features may preserve or restore app data depending on your device settings. Manage those backups through your device or backup provider. Clearing Unitify's data does not delete content already copied, shared, or saved in another app.

Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably needed to handle your request and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us. We cannot remotely access or delete information stored only on your device.

7. Security

Local app data is stored within the storage provided to Unitify by your operating system. Its protection also depends on your device security, screen lock, and backup settings. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

8. External links and services

Unitify may open websites, an email app, an app store, or a sharing destination when you choose those actions. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

This policy describes the Unitify app. The hosting provider for this policy page and other linked websites may process web requests under their own privacy policies.

9. Children's privacy

Unitify is a general-purpose conversion tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through support correspondence, please contact us so we can address the request and delete that information where appropriate.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent.
